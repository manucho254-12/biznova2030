{{ icon('award', 18) }} How it works

1
It opens on weekends

The Fun Zone opens every Friday, Saturday and Sunday (East Africa Time).

2
A topic is posted

The topic comes from the admin, or the system selects a member and notifies them on their dashboard to bring it.

3
Everyone chats

Members discuss the topic together in one shared chat under the Today's Chat tab.

4
A winner is rewarded

The day's winner receives KES {{ '%.0f'|format(prize) }} in their wallet.

{{ icon('shield-check', 18) }} House rules

  • Be respectful — no insults, hate speech or harassment.
  • No spam, links to other sites, or sharing of personal contact details.
  • The admin can see all messages, usernames and profile details, and may remove messages or suspend accounts that break the rules.
  • Add a profile picture and a short description on your Profile page.
