Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Fun Zone and how do I win?

The Fun Zone is our weekend community chat, open on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays. The admin opens each day's session with a topic, members join the conversation, and the admin selects one winner per day, who receives KES 100 in their wallet. Only activated members can take part.

Is BizNova free to join?

Creating an account is completely free. To unlock daily earning tasks and your referral link, you pay a one-time activation fee via M-Pesa.

How do I get paid?

Every task you complete adds to your BizNova wallet balance. Once your balance reaches the minimum threshold, you can request a withdrawal straight to your M-Pesa number.

How much do I earn per referral?

You earn a referral bonus for every friend who signs up with your referral link and activates their account, plus a smaller bonus when they go on to refer their own friends. Top weekly and monthly referrers also earn extra cash bonuses on the leaderboard.

What tasks can I do to earn money?

You can like photos and watch promotional videos from our partner companies, answer trivia questions, complete surveys, apply for our top-paying academic and professional writing tasks (paid in USD), and refer friends — all from your dashboard.

How long do withdrawals take to process?

Withdrawal requests are typically processed within 24-48 hours, after a small transaction cost that varies by amount.

Is my account and password safe?

Yes. Passwords are securely encrypted and never visible to anyone, including BizNova admins. Activation and withdrawals go through M-Pesa's secure payment system, and login includes email verification for extra protection.
