Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information BizNova ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to everyone who creates an account on the Platform.

1. Information we collect

When you register and use the Platform, we collect:

  • Account details: full name, username, email address, phone number, and password (stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text).
  • Payment information: M-Pesa transaction codes and phone numbers used for activation payments and withdrawals. We do not collect or store your M-Pesa PIN — payments are processed through PayHero, our payment processing partner.
  • Activity data: tasks completed (photo likes, video views, trivia answers, surveys, writing task submissions), wallet balance, referral relationships, and withdrawal history.
  • Technical data: IP address and basic request logs, used for security purposes such as rate-limiting login attempts and detecting fraud.

2. How we use your information

We use the information above to: create and operate your account; process activation payments and withdrawals via M-Pesa; credit task and referral earnings to your wallet; send you emails (email verification codes, login codes, password resets, referral and withdrawal notifications); communicate with you if you contact support; detect and prevent fraud or abuse of the Platform; and comply with legal obligations.

3. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited information with:

  • PayHero (our payment processor): your phone number and payment amount, to process activation payments and withdrawals via M-Pesa.
  • Our email delivery provider: your email address and message content, solely to deliver account-related emails.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of BizNova, our users, or others.

4. Data retention

We retain your account and transaction information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for record-keeping, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

5. Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and to object to certain uses of it. To exercise these rights, contact us at [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] or through "Contact Admin" on your dashboard. Note that deleting your account does not reverse completed transactions or erase records we are legally required to keep.

6. Cookies

We use a single essential session cookie to keep you logged in. We do not currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including password hashing, rate-limiting on login and verification attempts, and restricting sensitive actions to authenticated sessions. No online service can guarantee absolute security, and you're responsible for keeping your own password confidential.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Platform after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to biznova254@gmail.com.
